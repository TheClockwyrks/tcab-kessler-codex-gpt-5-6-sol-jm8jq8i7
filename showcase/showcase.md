# Kessler

The cleanup orbit has become a cascade. Ride the deflector around the planet, send the demolition ball back into the debris rings, and steer every return by choosing where it strikes your paddle.

Each wave packs three rings with derelicts. The outer bands counter-rotate, gaps drift out of alignment, and a missed return burns against the atmosphere. Clear the whole sky to bank a wave bonus and face a faster orbit.

Broken targets may shed salvage. Catch widening rigs, extra demolition balls, a one-hit shield, or a piercing charge—but read the pod before committing, because a narrowing rig is bad news.
